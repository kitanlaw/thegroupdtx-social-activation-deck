A Full Day of Competition

Summer Olympics is The Group DTX's flagship field day, now in its fourth year. Teams compete across a full day of games built for adults who want to play hard and laugh harder, all leading up to jerseys, medals, and a closing celebration that brings the whole group back together.

This isn't a generic corporate field day. It's the same community that sells out our trivia nights, mixers, and gallery events, brought outside for a full day of team competition, culture, and connection. Your brand shows up where the community already is.

How the Day Unfolds

01
Arrival & Registration

Teams check in and get jerseys as the energy starts building. Your brand can be the first thing they see, on badges, signage, or in the welcome swag.

02
Opening Ceremony

Teams are introduced and the day officially kicks off. This is where a sponsor gets a named mention and an MC shoutout in front of the full crowd.

03
Team Competitions

The events run back to back with downtime between heats for people to talk. Your branded station or product lives right on the field, all day.

04
Closing Celebration & Medals

Medals go out and the day wraps with everyone together. This is prime space for a branded moment and photo ops with the winning teams.
